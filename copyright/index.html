Respecting every creator.

This policy explains which materials belong to the JMB MEDIA project, how third-party material is presented, and how to report a copyright concern.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 Last updated: 16 November 2026

Important: JMB MEDIA is a media discovery directory and player interface. A stream, logo, article feed or other item being publicly reachable does not automatically mean that it is free to copy, redistribute, rebroadcast or commercially use.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the JMB MEDIA website and its sections: JMB TV, Newspapers, JMB Radio and JMB Social Media. It covers the site's branding and original design as well as third-party channel listings, programme streams, station information, publisher feeds, headlines, videos, audio and logos that may be referenced or displayed.

JMB MEDIA does not claim ownership of material merely because it appears in a directory, is linked from a page, or can be played through the site's player.

Unless a page or file says otherwise, the original JMB MEDIA name and logo, project-specific visual design, page layouts, original interface artwork and original website text are materials of the JMB MEDIA project. All rights in those original materials are reserved.

You may link to JMB MEDIA pages. Please do not copy, republish, sell, remove attribution from, or present the project's original branding or design as your own without permission from the relevant rights holder.

This section does not give JMB MEDIA ownership of third-party names, brands, channel identities, artwork, programmes, recordings or articles shown on the site.

3. Third-party material and ownership

JMB TV and programme streams

JMB TV uses public channel-directory information, including listings from IPTV-org and a supplemental public IPTV directory. Broadcasters and other rights holders retain their rights in television programmes, films, live broadcasts, music, channel names, logos and stream content.

JMB TV may connect directly to a provider's stream or use a technical proxy for compatible browser playback. This technical function does not give JMB MEDIA a licence to the programme and does not bypass subscriptions, DRM, logins, geographic restrictions or broadcaster access controls. Channel availability and permission to view or redistribute a stream are determined by the provider and applicable terms.

Newspapers and news feeds

The Newspapers and Social Media sections may display headlines, short summaries, images or other material supplied through publisher RSS/Atom feeds or source-linked news feeds. Publishers and their respective authors or rights holders retain copyright in their articles, photographs, graphics and other reporting. Where a feed supplies longer article text, that does not transfer ownership to JMB MEDIA.

Use the original publisher's page for the complete report, corrections, licensing terms and any reuse permission. JMB MEDIA does not grant permission to republish a publisher's article or photograph.

Radio, video, audio and logos

Radio station names, directory metadata, audio broadcasts, newsroom videos, podcasts, thumbnails, channel logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners or licensors. JMB MEDIA uses source names and identifying marks to help visitors recognise the relevant station, publisher or broadcaster. Their appearance does not imply sponsorship, partnership or endorsement.

You may browse the directory and follow links to the original providers for personal discovery. Any other use must be permitted by the relevant rights holder, the provider's terms and applicable law.

  • Do not download, record, copy, re-upload, sell, publicly perform, rebroadcast or redistribute third-party programmes, music, articles, photographs or streams unless you have permission or a valid legal basis.
  • Do not remove copyright notices, watermarks, credits or other rights information.

Official reference: Copyright Act, 1957 — Chapter XI (Sections 51 and 52).

5. Reporting a copyright concern

If you own rights in material or are authorised to represent the rights holder and believe that a specific JMB MEDIA page is using or linking to material in error, please identify the exact item so it can be reviewed.

Information that helps us review a report

  1. The exact JMB MEDIA page URL and the specific channel, feed item, image or other material concerned.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work and the rights you own or are authorised to represent.
  3. The original source URL, where available, and a short explanation of the concern.
  4. The action requested, such as checking a listing, correcting attribution, disabling a link or removing material.

For an initial, non-sensitive report, you can use the JMB MEDIA GitHub issue page. GitHub issues are public. Do not post identity documents, signatures, private addresses, phone numbers or confidential evidence there.

JMB MEDIA has not published a separate private copyright-notice email or form. Do not place sensitive legal or personal information in a public issue. For a formal notice that requires confidential details, use the relevant hosting platform's official private legal/reporting channel. A parent, guardian or responsible adult should help a minor with any formal legal notice.

Reports are reviewed on the information available. Where appropriate, JMB MEDIA may correct attribution, remove or disable a listing or link, or ask for additional non-sensitive information. Sending a report does not guarantee a particular outcome or an immediate removal.

6. Corrections and good-faith reports

If a listing is inaccurate, points to the wrong source, or appears to use material without permission, please report the specific page and item. If you believe a listing was disabled by mistake, explain the basis for your permission or other lawful use. Do not submit false, misleading or knowingly incomplete claims.
