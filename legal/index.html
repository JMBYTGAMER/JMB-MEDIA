JMB TV · SITE POLICIES

Respect the original source.

JMB TV is a discovery directory. It displays limited factual information and links visitors to third-party websites. It does not host, embed, record, cache, proxy, copy or retransmit television broadcasts, radio programmes, newspaper articles, photographs, news stories, videos or podcasts.

Last reviewed: 14 November 2026

✓
Directory-only design

JMB TV does not contain an in-page TV or radio player. Visitors leave JMB when they choose to open a broadcaster, publisher or station website. The destination controls its own content, access rules and terms.

1. What JMB TV displays

Television directory: channel names, channel IDs, network/owner names, country, language and category metadata, plus a website address where one is supplied by the data source. Channel artwork and programme media are not fetched or shown. JMB does not use the IPTV-org stream playlist or embed its stream URLs.

Newspapers and world news: publication/source names, basic directory information and links to original publisher websites. JMB does not fetch, store, reproduce, summarise or display publisher articles, headlines, photographs, thumbnails or video/audio content.

Radio: station directory information and source website links only. JMB does not play, host, proxy or retransmit station audio.

2. TV data sources and licences

JMB uses channel metadata from the IPTV-org API, whose channel records are sourced from the IPTV-org database. The API documentation identifies fields such as channel name, country, categories and an official website URL. JMB also uses descriptive country, language, category and exclusion metadata. The API and database repositories publish Unlicense/public-domain dedication terms for their repository contents. See the upstream licence files before reusing the data yourself.

JMB does not use the IPTV-org M3U playlist, streams API, logo API or third-party channel artwork in the TV directory. An upstream website field can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect; it is shown as a source-supplied link, not a guarantee that the destination is controlled by the broadcaster.

Upstream data licensing does not grant a licence to broadcaster programmes, channel logos, trademarks, music, films, photographs or other material hosted by a broadcaster. Those rights remain with their respective owners.

3. No rebroadcasting or access circumvention

JMB does not embed or play broadcast streams, use a media proxy, rewrite stream playlists, record broadcasts, bypass DRM, spoof referrers or user agents, evade paywalls, subscriptions, login requirements or geographic restrictions. JMB does not offer downloads of third-party media. A public link or a channel's presence in a directory is not proof that a broadcast is authorised for every person, territory or purpose.

In India, the Copyright Act, 1957 includes a separate broadcast reproduction right for broadcasting organisations. Do not use JMB to reproduce, rebroadcast or make a protected broadcast available without the necessary permission or licence. See Chapter VIII, including Section 37, and obtain qualified legal advice for a specific use.

4. Third-party websites, names and trademarks

Channel, network, publication and station names are used only to identify the subject of a directory entry. JMB is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by a broadcaster, publisher, radio station or data provider unless an explicit written agreement says otherwise. Third-party names, marks, logos and website content belong to their respective owners.

External websites open under their own control. They may show advertisements, require an account, collect visitor information, apply age or regional restrictions, or change their content and terms. Check that a destination is genuine before entering information or using its services.

5. Copyright, accuracy and removal requests

Directory information can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. JMB may remove a listing, hide a link or correct metadata when a credible complaint or error report is received. A rights holder or authorised representative can submit a request through the JMB MEDIA issue tracker. Include the JMB page URL, the affected entry and a public source URL only. Do not post private contact details, identity documents or confidential material in a public issue.

For confidential or formal legal correspondence, use the contact route available from the project owner's GitHub profile. A parent or guardian should handle formal legal correspondence for a minor. JMB may temporarily remove a disputed listing while reviewing a request.

6. Privacy and browser storage

The site does not require an account and does not ask visitors to submit payment information or publish user posts. JMB currently has no advertising or analytics tracker configured. The TV page stores only the visitor's favourite channel IDs and optional theme preference in local browser storage. Those preferences are not sent to JMB as an account profile.

The browser may connect to JMB hosting, public directory data providers, font providers and third-party websites opened by the visitor. Those providers may receive ordinary connection information such as IP address, browser details and requested URLs. JMB does not control their privacy practices; review their policies before visiting.

7. No legal guarantee

This policy describes JMB's current technical design. It is not legal advice and cannot guarantee that no complaint, copyright claim, trademark issue, privacy issue or other legal concern will ever arise. Laws and licences depend on facts and jurisdiction. Obtain permission from rights holders before using any third-party media, branding or other protected material, especially for commercial use or public distribution.
