JMB TV · SITE POLICIES

Respect the original source.

JMB TV is a media discovery hub. Its TV page displays channel metadata and publicly listed stream links from IPTV-org, and plays available streams directly in the visitor’s browser. JMB does not host or relay the video.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

✓
How the TV player works

JMB TV loads the public IPTV-org channel playlist and related channel metadata. When a visitor selects a channel, the browser connects directly to that listed stream and plays it in the JMB TV player when the source supports browser playback. JMB does not upload, record, store, proxy, rewrite or retransmit the video. Some listed streams may be offline, region-limited or unavailable in a browser.

1. What JMB TV provides

JMB TV provides search, filters, a channel catalogue and an in-page player for stream addresses published in the IPTV-org public playlist. The page loads the video directly from the stream host in the visitor's browser. JMB does not host or store programme video, create copies, or act as a media relay.

  • Television: worldwide channel metadata, logos and publicly listed stream addresses from IPTV-org. Available streams can play inside the JMB TV page. The directory is not a complete list of every broadcaster in the world; it reflects the channels currently published by its data source.
  • Radio: station directory information and source links only. JMB does not play or relay station audio.
  • Newspapers: publication names, regions, languages and links to publisher websites. JMB does not scrape or display article text, headlines, summaries or publisher images.
  • World News: directories of publisher websites, official video-channel pages and podcast pages. JMB does not copy news stories, thumbnails or media.

2. Copyright and broadcast rights

The IPTV-org project describes its playlist as a collection of publicly available channel streams submitted by contributors and says that, to the best of its knowledge, those links were intentionally made public by copyright holders. JMB uses the public playlist as a discovery source; this is not a guarantee that every individual stream is authorised for every viewer, territory or use.

Third-party programmes, logos, music and broadcasts remain the property of their respective right holders. JMB does not claim ownership, partnership, sponsorship or endorsement. A working public URL or catalogue listing does not by itself create a licence for commercial use, recording, redistribution or rebroadcasting.

JMB connects the browser directly to the listed stream host. It does not use a CORS proxy, hide the source host, bypass DRM, paywalls, authentication, subscriptions or geographic restrictions. When a stream cannot be played directly, the player displays an error rather than relaying it through another server.

3. Third-party data and attribution

TV channel catalogue: JMB reads the public playlist from IPTV-org/iptv and channel metadata from the IPTV-org API. The metadata API provides channel, feed, logo, country, language, category and blocklist information. IPTV-org states that its playlist stores links rather than video files and that the links point to publicly available streams submitted by contributors.

The IPTV-org project licence applies to its own repository contents; it does not grant JMB rights to broadcaster programmes, logos, trademarks or third-party websites. Channel information may be incomplete or outdated. NSFW and blocklisted channel entries are excluded where the source metadata identifies them.

Radio station directory: descriptive station metadata from Radio Browser. Newspapers and world-news links point to their respective publishers. When a visitor opens a third-party stream or website, that provider's own terms, privacy policy and access restrictions apply.

4. No bypassing or restricted access

JMB TV only attempts direct browser playback of the stream address listed in the public directory. It does not use proxy services, rewrite playlists, spoof referrers or user agents, bypass DRM, paywalls, subscriptions, authentication, disabled playback or geographic restrictions. Some channels will therefore not work on every device or network. Do not use JMB to avoid a source provider's payment or access conditions.

5. Copyright or content-removal requests

A rights holder or authorised representative who believes a JMB directory entry is inaccurate or should be removed can request review through the JMB MEDIA issue tracker. Include only the affected JMB page and source URL in a public issue; do not post private contact details, identity documents or confidential material there.

For a formal legal notice or confidential supporting documents, use the private contact route available through the project owner's GitHub profile. A parent or guardian should handle formal legal correspondence for a minor. JMB may temporarily hide a disputed link while it is reviewed and may remove links that are blocked by the data provider or subject to a credible rights complaint.

6. Privacy and local storage

JMB does not require an account and does not ask visitors to submit personal information, payment details or posts. The site currently has no advertising or analytics tracker configured.

Your browser may store site preferences, the light/dark theme, radio-directory favourites and a cached app shell for installation/offline navigation. These items are stored in your browser or device and are not sent to JMB as an account profile.

When a page requests public directory data, fonts or a third-party website, the relevant provider may receive normal connection information such as your IP address, browser details and requested URL. JMB does not control those providers' data handling. Review their privacy policies before opening external links.

7. Accuracy and limitations

Directory names and website addresses can be incomplete, outdated, region-specific or changed by their owners. A link is not a guarantee that a service is available, free, licensed for every viewer or endorsed by JMB. Check the source's own terms and local rules before use.

This policy is an explanation of JMB's design choices, not legal advice and not a guarantee that every third-party link is free of disputes. Copyright and broadcasting rules can depend on jurisdiction, facts, licences and intended use. Obtain qualified legal advice and written permissions before any commercial use, monetisation, embedding or distribution.
