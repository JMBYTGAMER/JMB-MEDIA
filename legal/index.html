JMB TV · SITE POLICIES

Respect the original source.

JMB TV is designed as a directory and navigation hub. The safest approach is to identify sources, link to their own websites and leave their content under their control.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

✓
Our content rule

JMB does not host, record, rebroadcast, proxy, embed, download, translate or republish third-party television, radio, newspaper, video or podcast content. We link visitors to the source's own website.

1. What JMB TV provides

JMB TV provides navigation tools, search, filters, basic directory labels and links to third-party websites. The website does not provide a television or radio broadcast service, newspaper archive, social network, video platform or podcast player.

  • Television: a channel-name directory with HTTPS website addresses supplied by the channel-data provider. There is no in-page stream player.
  • Radio: station directory information and HTTPS station-homepage links only. JMB does not play or relay station audio.
  • Newspapers: publication names, regions, languages and links to publisher websites. JMB does not scrape or display article text, headlines, summaries or publisher images.
  • World News: directories of publisher websites, official video-channel pages and podcast pages. JMB does not copy news stories, thumbnails or media.

2. Copyright and broadcast rights

Third-party articles, photographs, video, audio, music, logos, broadcasts and other creative material may be protected by copyright, related rights, trademarks or website terms. Public accessibility, a working URL, or inclusion in a community directory is not itself a licence for JMB to rebroadcast or republish that material.

India's Copyright Act, 1957 includes a separate broadcast reproduction right for broadcasting organisations. Section 37 addresses acts such as rebroadcasting a broadcast without the right-holder's licence. JMB therefore does not embed or relay unverified TV or radio streams.

Channel and publisher names are used only to identify the source being linked. Their owners retain their respective rights. JMB does not claim ownership, partnership, sponsorship or endorsement.

3. Third-party data and attribution

The channel directory uses channel metadata from the IPTV-org API. Its project documentation describes the channel website field and its blocklist; its repository publishes an Unlicense for the API project. That project licence does not grant rights to broadcaster programmes, trademarks or third-party websites.

The radio station directory uses descriptive station metadata from Radio Browser. Its API documentation describes the API as free and open source. This does not give JMB a right to play, redistribute or rebroadcast the listed station audio.

Publisher, newsroom, video-channel and podcast links point to their respective source websites. When a visitor opens a third-party website, that provider's own terms, privacy policy, access restrictions and copyright rules apply.

4. No bypassing or restricted access

JMB does not bypass paywalls, subscriptions, authentication, DRM, geographic restrictions, access controls, disabled embeds or a provider's technical restrictions. It does not offer playlists of stream URLs, media proxying or download functionality. Visitors must use the source's own service and must not use JMB to avoid payment or access conditions.

5. Copyright or content-removal requests

A rights holder or authorised representative who believes a JMB directory entry is inaccurate or should be removed can request review through the JMB MEDIA issue tracker. Include only the affected JMB page and source URL in a public issue; do not post private contact details, identity documents or confidential material there.

For a formal legal notice or confidential supporting documents, use the private contact route available through the project owner's GitHub profile. A parent or guardian should handle formal legal correspondence for a minor. JMB may temporarily hide a disputed link while it is reviewed and may remove links that are blocked by the data provider or subject to a credible rights complaint.

6. Privacy and local storage

JMB does not require an account and does not ask visitors to submit personal information, payment details or posts. The site currently has no advertising or analytics tracker configured.

Your browser may store site preferences, the light/dark theme, radio-directory favourites and a cached app shell for installation/offline navigation. These items are stored in your browser or device and are not sent to JMB as an account profile.

When a page requests public directory data, fonts or a third-party website, the relevant provider may receive normal connection information such as your IP address, browser details and requested URL. JMB does not control those providers' data handling. Review their privacy policies before opening external links.

7. Accuracy and limitations

Directory names and website addresses can be incomplete, outdated, region-specific or changed by their owners. A link is not a guarantee that a service is available, free, licensed for every viewer or endorsed by JMB. Check the source's own terms and local rules before use.

This policy is an explanation of JMB's design choices, not legal advice and not a guarantee that every third-party link is free of disputes. Copyright and broadcasting rules can depend on jurisdiction, facts, licences and intended use. Obtain qualified legal advice and written permissions before any commercial use, monetisation, embedding or distribution.
