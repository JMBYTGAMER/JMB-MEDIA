# JMB Radio tuner — frequency reference and playback behavior

The FM1, FM2, AM1 and AM2 banks are reference presets for Kerala broadcast services. The numbers are not invented demo frequencies.

## Official frequency references

- The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting's private FM channel list (31 July 2026) includes the Kochi private FM frequencies used for Radio Mango, Red FM, Club FM and Radio Mirchi, and the Thiruvananthapuram and Thrissur listings used in FM2.
- Prasar Bharati's Akashvani station list includes Kerala medium-wave and FM transmitters such as Alappuzha 576 kHz, Kozhikode 684 kHz, Thrissur 630 kHz, Kochi FM 102.3 MHz, Rainbow Kochi 107.5 MHz, Punalur FM 100.1 MHz, Thrissur FM 101.1 MHz and Thiruvananthapuram MW 1161 kHz.

The bank names are JMB's saved preset groups. They are not four separate physical receivers.

## Important playback distinction

A website cannot operate a phone's built-in FM/AM tuner. JMB Radio therefore uses the broadcast frequencies as a tuning reference and separately looks for a matching, public HTTPS internet audio stream through Radio Browser. A preset is playable only when a matching stream is found. The broadcaster may not publish an online stream, and an online stream may be offline, changed or unavailable in a particular browser.

Use the Radio directory below the tuner to search for a station manually when no matching preset stream is found.
